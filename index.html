Five projects, five different questions

Each one started as a raw, uncooperative dataset. Here's what I found once I stopped asking "what happened" and started asking "what should change."

Healthcare Analytics
Hospital Readmission Analysis

Analyzed hospital patient data to identify the primary drivers of readmission and support strategic interventions to reduce avoidable hospital returns — a metric directly tied to cost, care quality, and operational strain.

Engineered a Comorbidity Score and Total Previous Visits feature. Found patients aged 70+ and those with higher comorbidity counts carried significantly higher readmission risk — with medication changes correlating to reduced readmissions.
Python (Pandas, NumPy) Excel Power BI Feature Engineering
SQL / E-Commerce
E-Commerce Data Analysis

Analyzed ~99,000 transactions across customers, orders, payments, and sellers to uncover revenue drivers, retention patterns, and structural risk hiding behind strong order volume.

49.42% of orders used installment payments; most customers never returned after one purchase; revenue rode heavily on a handful of categories and sellers.
SQL (MySQL) Python Matplotlib
Power BI / Finance
Credit Card Analytics Dashboard

Built a two-page executive dashboard covering customer segmentation and transaction behavior — demographics, spend channels, card tiers, and revenue concentration.

Self-employed customers outnumbered every other job segment; Swipe transactions drove 62.6% of revenue; Blue-tier cards alone generated 38M vs. 1M for Platinum.
Power BI DAX Power Query
Power BI / HR
HR Attrition Dashboard

Built a dashboard to monitor employee attrition across departments and surface the operational factors most correlated with people leaving.

Overtime, tenure, job role, and job satisfaction emerged as the clearest attrition drivers — feeding directly into workforce planning recommendations.
Power BI Data Analysis
Python / Mobile Apps
Play Store App Analysis

Explored category and rating trends across a large set of Google Play Store listings to understand what separates consistently well-rated apps from the rest.

Cleaned and structured the dataset in Pandas, visualized early patterns in Matplotlib, then built an interactive Power BI view of category-level rating trends.
Python (Pandas) Matplotlib Power BI
